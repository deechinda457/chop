Jollof Rice with Grilled Chicken

⏱ 35 min🍽 Serves 2📊 Easy
NigerianOne-potUses 5 pantry items

Ingredients

2 cups rice
4 tomatoes, blended
1 onion, sliced
500g chicken
2 tbsp palm oil

Steps

1
Season chicken and grill until golden, about 15 minutes.
2
Blend tomatoes, pepper, and onion into a smooth base.
3
Fry the base in palm oil until reduced and deep red.
4
Add rice and stock, cover, and simmer until tender.
